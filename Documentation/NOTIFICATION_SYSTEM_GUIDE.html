Daily Status Reminders

01_daily_status_reminders.xlsx

📋 Purpose

Remind vendors to submit their daily attendance status to ensure 100% compliance

👥 Recipients

Vendors

📱 Delivery Method

🟣 Microsoft Teams

⏰ Trigger Conditions

  • Every 3 hours from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
  • Only on weekdays (Monday-Friday)
  • Only to vendors who haven't submitted status for current day
  • Stops automatically when vendor submits their status
  • Maximum 3 reminders per day per vendor
⚙️ Configurable Options
  • Reminder intervals (default: 3 hours)
  • Start/end times (default: 9 AM - 6 PM)
  • Maximum reminders per day
  • Weekdays only setting
  • Holiday exclusions
  • Custom message templates
  • Auto-escalation to managers

📋 Purpose

👥 Recipients

Vendors
Managers
Admins

  • Resolution steps included
  • Acknowledgment requirements
  • Alert immediately setting
  • Include resolution steps

🔧 Smart Notification Features

⚡

Real-Time Database Sync

Notification recipients automatically update when users are added, removed, or roles change. No manual intervention required.

🎯

Intelligent Filtering

Time-aware, holiday-aware, role-based, and status-aware filtering ensures notifications reach the right people at the right time.

📱

Teams-First Delivery

All notifications delivered through Microsoft Teams for instant, centralized communication. Optimized for workplace collaboration and real-time engagement.

📊

Excel Configuration

All notification settings managed through Excel files. Non-technical users can easily modify timing, content, and recipients.

🔍

Monitoring & Validation

Continuous health checks, sync validation, and auto-repair ensure the notification system runs reliably 24/7.

🌐

Power Automate Ready

Full webhook integration with Microsoft Power Automate for advanced automation workflows and business logic.
